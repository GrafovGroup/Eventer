## 0. Mission and non-negotiables
Act as a coordinated team of Canadian B2B event-industry strategist, researcher, brand director, UX designer, bilingual copywriter, SEO/AEO specialist, accessibility lead, developer, QA tester, and release manager. Build a premium, fast, conversion-focused, Canada-wide event-services website. EVENTER is the direct client-facing provider and accountable project coordinator, never a directory, lead broker, or referral marketplace. Specialist subcontractors may fulfil work; do not claim in-house capabilities, owned inventory, offices, certifications, client work, insurance, or staffing without verification. No fabricated testimonials, portfolio images labelled as actual work, client logos, prices, availability, awards, or performance claims. Do not add AI-generated-site credits, image-generation explanations, or Grafov Group branding to public pages. Respect licensing and privacy.

## 1. Approved brand system
Use approved Creative Contemporary concept #5, with three treatments: #1 Signature primary horizontal logo; #4 icon-focused stacked/icon-only treatment; #5 dark-background treatment. Colourful glossy ribbon lowercase 'e', deep navy EVENTER wordmark on light surfaces, white wordmark on dark surfaces, small Canadian maple leaf secondary accent. Tagline ONLY: 'EVERYTHING EVENTS.' Never use 'One Provider' anywhere, including image copy, metadata, alt text, or assets. Reference mockups are conceptual, not vector masters: reconstruct clean SVG logo geometry with designer review; export SVG, transparent PNG at multiple sizes, dark/light/monochrome variants, favicon, app icon and OG image. Keep logo legible at small sizes; do not bake the service list into the core mark. Proposed colour tokens: navy #07152F, white #FFFFFF, cyan #00C9F4, blue #175CFF, violet #7437F5, magenta #EE24AD, orange #FF7B1A, red #F32638. ## 2. Research and positioning (Phase 0)
Before coding, research current Canadian corporate event planning, AV production, event rentals, exhibit services, logistics, and nationwide providers. Summarize competitors, customer segments, search intent, differentiators, service feasibility, compliance considerations, and market gaps with dated sources and no invented numbers. Prioritize corporations, agencies, trade shows, convention centres, hospitality, municipalities, institutions, retail activations and festivals. Promise coordinated planning-to-execution and a single accountable contact without reverting to the removed 'One Provider' slogan. Use quote-led commercial positioning, not a public price catalogue. Explain bundled event solutions and complementary-service cross-selling.

## 3. Services: exhaustive taxonomy with dedicated pages
Create service hubs and unique detail pages, where commercially credible, for:
- Event planning & management: corporate events, conferences, conventions, meetings, galas, awards, product launches, brand activations, public events, festivals, institutional events, event coordination.
- Rentals: stanchions, ropes, retractable belts, crowd barriers, temporary fencing, tables, chairs, lounge furniture, carpets, red carpets, artificial turf, tents, canopies, pipe-and-drape, partitions, coat racks, rolling racks, iPads/tablets, registration counters, display equipment, signage, portable ramps, temporary flooring, queue management, accessories.
- Production: staging, risers, sound/PA, microphones, lighting, LED walls, projection, video, livestream coordination, truss/rigging coordination, show calling and technical crews.
- Trade shows/exhibitions: booths, registration, display structures, floor covering, partitions, furniture, installation, dismantling, logistics.
- Infrastructure: barricades, fencing, wayfinding, temporary access, safety equipment, traffic/pedestrian management support, event site infrastructure.
- Logistics and on-site services: delivery, installation, teardown, event changeovers, storage coordination, load-in/out, staffing and event support.
For regulated, safety-critical, electrical, structural, fire, accessibility, or rigging services, require qualified execution and relevant local approvals; do not imply approvals are included. Do not publish services as currently available until operationally validated.
Every service page: unique useful hero image, clear scope, applications, inclusions/exclusions, planning inputs, cross-sells, relevant FAQ, service area, CTA, unique metadata, and accessible breadcrumbs.

English and Québec-appropriate French from day one, including navigation, forms, validation, legal content, metadata, alt text, and success/error messages. Use language-specific routes, canonical/hreflang (en-CA, fr-CA, x-default where justified), translated slugs when maintainable, and working switch retaining page context. Include every province/territory and strategically prioritized city/metropolitan pages: Toronto/GTA (including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Newmarket), Ottawa, Montréal, Québec City, Vancouver/Metro Vancouver, Victoria, Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg, Halifax, and other relevant centres. Publish location pages only when differentiated by genuinely useful local event needs, service logistics, venues/markets or operational facts; no mass doorway pages, invented local offices, fake service guarantees, or duplicate city images. Add location coverage architecture that can scale safely.

## 8. SEO, AEO and technical search
Perform keyword mapping by service, event type, industry and location. Implement unique title/meta, one H1, semantic sections, internal links, canonical, robots.txt, sitemap index and per-language sitemaps, 301 redirects for changed URLs, Open Graph/Twitter cards, JSON-LD Organization/Service/BreadcrumbList/FAQPage only where truthful and valid, accessible FAQs and concise answer-oriented summaries. Never promise FAQ rich results, ranking, or indexing. Provide Search Console/Bing Webmaster verification checklist and exact sitemap URL(s); only claim submission if verified. No invented review ratings or local business addresses. Check noindex/canonical/hreflang conflicts and robots/sitemap crawlability. Build scalable content architecture rather than generating thousands of thin pages.

## 9. UX, accessibility and performance
Responsive 320px to ultrawide; use fluid max-width containers, readable line lengths, consistent gutters, no horizontal scroll, touch targets, keyboard nav, focus indicators, skip link, semantic headings, contrast, WCAG 2.2 AA target, reduced motion, accessible forms. Ensure all menus, CTA links, phone/email, EN/FR switch, images and quote flows work at mobile/tablet/desktop. Performance: optimize LCP hero, responsive image sizes, font loading, lazy loading below fold, code splitting, caching and minimal third-party scripts. Measure Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals and fix actionable issues; report actual scores rather than asserting success.
