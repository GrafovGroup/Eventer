# EVENTER — All Event Types and Audiences
Status: approved strategic scope, subject to actual fulfillment validation.
Brand: EVENTER — EVERYTHING EVENTS.

## Positioning
Serve corporate, private, public, community, cultural, institutional and entertainment events nationwide. Do not position EVENTER as exclusively B2B. Keep corporate/commercial opportunities prominent without excluding consumers and families. Direct accountable event-service provider, not a directory or lead broker. Qualified specialty partners may execute work; verify before claiming availability.

## Event-type architecture
- Corporate and business: conferences, meetings, seminars, team events, corporate galas, awards, product launches, brand activations, holiday parties.
- Weddings and celebrations: weddings, receptions, engagements, anniversaries, birthdays, baby showers, family celebrations, milestone parties.
- Concerts and festivals: live music, cultural festivals, street festivals, outdoor entertainment, food festivals.
- Trade shows and exhibitions: expos, conventions, consumer shows, pop-ups, exhibits.
- Community and municipal: parades, civic ceremonies, public celebrations, community gatherings.
- Sports and recreation: tournaments, races, competitions, fan zones, spectator events.
- Education and institutions: graduations, orientations, alumni events, academic conferences, school functions.
- Charity and nonprofit: fundraisers, benefit galas, auctions, charity races.
- Religious and cultural: ceremonies, religious gatherings, multicultural festivals; use respectful and inclusive copy.
- Luxury and VIP: private galas, receptions, premium hospitality.
- Film and entertainment: premieres, red carpets, production support, press events.
- Virtual and hybrid: online conferences, livestreams, hybrid events.
- Seasonal and holiday: winter events, holiday markets, New Year's Eve, summer celebrations.

## Required implementation
Add EVENT TYPES hub and differentiated detail pages for each major category. Each page should include audience, relevant services, equipment packages (without invented pricing), planning questions, unique appropriate imagery, FAQs, cross-sells and REQUEST A QUOTE. Include all categories in EN/FR navigation, search metadata, sitemap, internal linking, quote-form event-type selector and lead routing. Do not create thin city x event-type combinations.

## Cross-sell examples
Wedding -> rentals, decor, catering coordination, photography, entertainment, transportation, cleanup.
Conference -> AV, staging, registration tablets, photography, security, staffing.
Festival -> barricades, tents, sanitation, security, first aid, signage, cleanup.
Sports event -> barriers, staging, PA, tents, crowd support, photo/video.
Film premiere -> carpet, stanchions, lighting, photography, security, logistics.

## Operational guardrails
No claim of available services, inventory, licenses, approvals, owned equipment or proven project portfolio without verification. Security, liquor service, food, medical, drone, transport, electrical, structural and rigging work must be handled by properly qualified providers. Validate insurance, permissions and municipal requirements per event.

## Priority rollout
Tier 1: corporate events, weddings, conferences, trade shows, festivals, private parties.
Tier 2: municipal, sports, institutional, charity, cultural, luxury, film.
Tier 3: virtual/hybrid, seasonal and specialized niches.
Prioritization guides build sequencing, not limits to EVENTER's offering.
