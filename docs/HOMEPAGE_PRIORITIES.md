# EVENTER Homepage Priorities — Approved
Date: 2026-10-28

## Positioning
Corporate events are the first featured event category, while EVENTER serves all types of events nationwide. Keep the tagline exactly: EVERYTHING EVENTS.

## Recommended hierarchy
1. Hero: EVERYTHING EVENTS. — Event planning, rentals, production and support for corporate, private and public events across Canada. REQUEST A QUOTE and EXPLORE EVENTS.
2. First featured category: Corporate Events & Conferences, including meetings, galas, awards, product launches and corporate celebrations.
3. Weddings & Private Celebrations.
4. Festivals, Concerts & Public Events.
5. Trade Shows & Exhibitions.
6. Event Rentals & Production.
7. Ancillary Services: security, photography, videography, staffing, catering coordination, entertainment, cleaning, technology, logistics and safety.
8. How It Works, nationwide coverage, FAQs and closing quote CTA.

## Design requirements
Corporate should be first in event-type cards and navigation ordering, but do not imply that EVENTER is corporate-only. Give other event categories substantial visual presence. Distinct relevant imagery for each category. EN/FR parity, responsive wide containers, accessible CTAs, and no 'One Provider' wording.

## Scope and compliance
Keep all event types from docs/EVENT_TYPES_SCOPE.md. No unverified availability, portfolio, owned inventory, licensed personnel or local office claims.
