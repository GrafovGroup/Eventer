# EVENTER Phase 0 — Research and Commercial Architecture
Date: 2026-10-28
Status: Initial research and proposed strategy; operational validation pending.

## Positioning
EVENTER — EVERYTHING EVENTS.
Canada-wide, bilingual, direct-accountability commercial event planning, rentals, production, installation and logistics. Primary audience: corporations, agencies, conventions, exhibitions, institutions, municipalities, hospitality and festivals.

## Competitor scan (initial, not exhaustive)
1. Encore Canada — event technology, production, exhibit/tradeshow and creative services. https://www.encore-can.com/ and https://www.encoreglobal.com/what-we-do/production-services/
2. Element Event Solutions — furniture, tenting, party rentals and drape/decor, national positioning. https://elementeventscanada.com/ ; https://www.linkedin.com/company/element-event-solutions/
3. GES — exhibition and trade-show services; Canadian locations in Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal, Edmonton and Banff/Lake Louise. https://www.ges.com/ca/service-locations/
Research finding: categories overlap, so avoid unsupported claims of uniqueness. Test positioning around multi-category convenience, clear scoping, responsive quotes, and execution coordination. Further research needed: regional competition, pricing models, search demand, procurement needs and client interviews.

## Priority segments and landing pages
1. Corporate events and conferences: recurring account potential; cross-sell AV, staging, furniture, registration.
2. Trade shows and exhibitions: cross-sell booths, carpet, stanchions, iPads, signs, logistics.
3. Municipal/institutional events: barriers, tents, access, temporary infrastructure, staffing.
4. Galas and brand activations: furniture, decor coordination, lighting, carpet, stage.
5. Festivals and outdoor events: tents, barriers, fencing, logistics and qualified installation.

## Cross-selling opportunities
Rental inquiry -> delivery, setup/teardown, staging, queue management.
Trade show -> carpet, stanchions, registration tablets, signage, booth installation.
Conference -> AV, furniture, registration, staging, logistics.
Outdoor event -> barriers, tenting, temporary flooring, site logistics.

## Guardrails
No claims of owned equipment, local offices, certified crews, specific delivery reach, portfolio, client names or fixed rates without verification. Do not publicly identify sister brands or Grafov Group without approval. Avoid creating false local presence.
